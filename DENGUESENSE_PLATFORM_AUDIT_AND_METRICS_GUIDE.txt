1. EXECUTIVE PLATFORM SUMMARY
================================================================================
DengueSense is a public-health decision support and environmental early warning
system covering all 64 districts of Bangladesh. It pairs daily meteorological
telemetry (temperature, rainfall accumulation, relative humidity, air quality)
with PostGIS spatial boundaries and citizen science community reporting.

Core Architectural Principles:
1. Strict Role Boundaries: Unauthenticated visitors see only the welcome/auth
   gate; citizens monitor risk and submit reports; moderators verify evidence;
   administrators manage the data pipeline and user access.
2. Signal Separation: Meteorological suitability (EDSI) is strictly separated
   from citizen crowd reports to prevent confounding environmental suitability
   with clinical infection probabilities.
3. Privacy by Design: Citizen report coordinates are systematically generalized
   to 2 decimal places (~1.1 km resolution) to protect reporter privacy.
4. Full Bilingual Parity: Complete 1-click toggle between English and Bangla (বাংলা).

[PAGE] / — National Environmental Dashboard
- Purpose: High-level situational awareness across Bangladesh's 64 districts.
- Permitted Roles: Citizen, Moderator, Admin.
- Key Metrics Displayed:
  1. "Total Districts" (Value: 64)
     - Explanation: All 64 official ADM2 administrative districts of Bangladesh.
     - Provenance: geoBoundaries ADM2 / Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics (BBS).
  2. "High Suitability" (e.g. 31)
     - Explanation: Count of districts where EDSI is between 55.0 and 74.9.
     - Meaning: Meteorological conditions strongly favor rapid Aedes mosquito
       breeding and shortened virus extrinsic incubation period (EIP).
  3. "Critical Suitability" (e.g. 0)
     - Explanation: Count of districts where EDSI exceeds 75.0.
     - Meaning: Extreme meteorological favorability; urgent vector control needed.
  4. "Ingestion Status" (Value: success / 64 districts updated)
     - Explanation: Health state of the daily automated Open-Meteo telemetry pipeline.
  5. "Interactive 64-District Choropleth Map":
     - Colored by EDSI: Green (Low), Yellow (Moderate), Orange (High), Red (Critical).
  6. "District Quick-Drawer":
     - Mean Temperature: 24-hour mean temp (°C) from Open-Meteo ERA5/Seamless.
     - Relative Humidity: Mean percentage (%) of atmospheric moisture.
     - 21-Day Rainfall: Cumulative precipitation (mm) over the past 3 weeks.
     - Community Signal Score: 0–100 score derived from approved citizen reports.
     - EDSI Factor Breakdown: Visual bars showing rainfall, temp, and humidity weights.
     - Air Quality Context: PM2.5 (µg/m³) and US AQI (stored for research).
  7. "Recent Community Reports Feed":
     - Last 5 verified reports with type badge, district, relative time, and upvotes.

4. SCIENTIFIC METRIC DEFINITIONS & FORMULAS (EDSI V1)
================================================================================

1. Environmental Dengue Suitability Index (EDSI V1):
   Scale: 0.0 to 100.0
   Formula:
     EDSI = 100 * [ (0.4375 * R_norm) + (0.3125 * T_norm) + (0.2500 * H_norm) ]

   Component Weights & Biological Rationale:
   a) Rainfall (R_norm, Weight: 43.75%):
      - Metric: 21-day lagged cumulative rainfall in millimeters.
      - Biology: Aedes aegypti eggs hatch in freshwater pools created by rainfall.
        A 21-day lag accounts for pool formation, larval instar development, pupation,
        and female adult maturation.
      - Scoring: Optimal breeding range is 80 mm – 250 mm (scores 1.0).
        Below 20 mm is dry (scores 0.1); above 400 mm flushes larvae (scores 0.4).

   b) Temperature (T_norm, Weight: 31.25%):
      - Metric: Daily mean temperature in degrees Celsius (°C).
      - Biology: Governs gonotrophic cycle speed and Extrinsic Incubation Period (EIP)
        of the dengue virus inside the female mosquito.
      - Scoring: Bell-curve peaking at 28°C (scores 1.0). Highly suitable between
        25°C and 32°C. Below 15°C or above 38°C mosquito mortality rises sharply.

   c) Relative Humidity (H_norm, Weight: 25.00%):
      - Metric: Daily relative humidity percentage (%).
      - Biology: High humidity prevents adult mosquito desiccation, extending
        lifespan and enabling multiple infectious blood meals.
      - Scoring: Linear ramp from 50% up to 85%+ (scores 1.0 above 80%).

2. Community Signal Score (CSS):
   Scale: 0.0 to 100.0
   Biology & Mechanism:
   - Separate from meteorological EDSI.
   - Calculated per district from approved citizen reports.
   - Freshness Decay: Each report loses weight over 14 days following an exponential
     decay: W(t) = e^(-0.1 * days_since_submission).
   - Upvotes add corroboration weight: W_total = W(t) * (1 + 0.2 * upvotes).
   - Suspected dengue reports are strictly non-clinical and quarantined from EDSI.
